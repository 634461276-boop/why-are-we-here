# PROCESS

## Tools Used and Purposes**
During this essay, I utilized the following tools:

* **Midjourney**: Used for brainstorming and visual testing. As detailed in this essay, I attempted to use Midjourney to generate a series of photos featuring a Chinese father and daughter at different stages of their lives. The immense generative power of the tool, combined with the profound sense of friction I experienced while trying to maintain character consistency, served as the primary inspiration for this essay.
* **Generative AI (e.g. ChatGPT/Gemini)**: Used for structuring,drafting, and editing. After experiencing the agonizing cycle of constant debugging with Midjourney, I used a large language model to help organize my scattered thoughts into a coherent framework and refine my vocabulary for the final draft.

## What I Kept and Why
* **What it generated that I kept**: During the drafting phase, the AI assistant helped articulate a specific metaphor regarding the human role:"you are the cartographer of the entire 'possibility space'".
* **Why it is good**: I kept this phrase because it perfectly elevates the core argument of the  essay. It beautifully captures the essence of the protracted tug-of-war with the machine. It accurately reflects the idea that when machines take over the assembly of pixels, the human creator is not a mere bystander, but someone who provides precise guidance and sets constraints to define the boundaries of the machine's output.
